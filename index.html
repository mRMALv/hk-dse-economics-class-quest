⚖️ 核心法律定義速覽 | Core Legal Definitions

掌握以下三項核心定義以區分私營企業 | Master these 3 core definitions
1. 獨立法人 | Separate Legal Entity

企業在法律上是一個獨立的個體，可以企業自身的名義擁有財產、簽訂合同及參與訴訟（包括起訴別人和被人起訴）。（註：獨資和合夥並不是法人，東主須以個人名義行事。）

An entity with an independent legal identity that can own property, sign contracts, and sue or be sued in its own name. (Note: Sole proprietorships and partnerships are not separate legal entities.)

2. 無限債務責任 | Unlimited Liability

東主的債務責任不限於其投資在廠商的金額。東主須對廠商的債務負上全部責任，可能要動用個人資產來償還廠商的債務。

Owners' liability for the firm's debts is not limited to the amount they have invested in the firm; they bear full responsibility and may have to use personal assets to repay the firm's debts.

3. 有限債務責任 | Limited Liability

東主（股東）的債務責任僅限於其投資在廠商的金額。由於有限公司是獨立法人，由公司自行承擔所有債務責任，股東毋須動用個人資產來償還有限公司的債務。

Owners' (shareholders') liability for the firm's debts is limited to the amount they have invested in the firm; as a separate legal entity, the company bears its own debts and shareholders do not need to use personal assets.
